## Stage 1.1 — Project Vision (Foundation)

Before designing the world, we define the game's identity.

### Working Project Name

Use a temporary codename until we find the perfect title.

**Codename:** Project Earth

### Vision

> "Create a realistic, living, Earth-inspired open-world game where every NPC has a purpose, every city feels alive, and players are free to build their own life through exploration, work, travel, business, and adventure."

## Core Design Principles

* 🌍 Earth-inspired geography (original, not a copy of real maps)
* 🚶 Freedom to explore anywhere
* 🧠 Intelligent NPCs with daily routines
* 💼 Multiple careers and businesses
* 🏠 Buy, rent, or build homes
* 🚗 Realistic driving and traffic
* 🌦️ Dynamic weather and seasons
* 🌐 Expandable world over time
* 💰 No pay-to-win mechanics

### Important Design Choice

Rather than recreating Earth exactly, we'll design an **Earth-inspired original world**. This lets us capture the feeling of traveling across continents and cultures while avoiding copyright and licensing issues with copying proprietary maps or branded locations.

In our next step (**Stage 1.2 – Planet & World Design**), we'll design the planet itself, including its continents, oceans, climate zones, and the rules that make the world feel alive before we build any cities.
