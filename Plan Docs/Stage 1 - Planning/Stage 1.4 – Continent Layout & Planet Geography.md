# Stage 1.4 – Continent Layout & Planet Geography

## Goal

Design a believable Earth-like planet where:

* Continents have realistic shapes.
* Climate feels natural.
* Rivers flow correctly.
* Mountains influence weather.
* Cities have logical locations.
* Future gameplay (roads, transport, economy, wildlife, missions) fits naturally.

Many game developers start by establishing **tectonics → mountains → rivers → climate → biomes → settlements**, because each layer influences the next. This workflow is widely recommended in worldbuilding resources.

---

# Step 1 — Planet Rules

We'll keep physics familiar:

* Earth-sized rocky planet
* 24-hour day
* 365-day year
* One moon
* Axial tilt similar to Earth
* Four seasons
* Dynamic weather

---

They will determine:

---

# Step 5 — River Network

A realistic river system follows simple rules:

* Rivers begin in mountains or lakes.
* They flow downhill.
* Smaller streams merge into larger rivers.
* Rivers usually end in seas or oceans.
* Large rivers often create fertile plains.

This helps determine farming areas, towns, bridges, and transport routes.

# Step 8 — Free Planning Resources

We'll rely on open resources for inspiration and planning:

* **Natural Earth** – Public-domain geographic datasets.
* **OpenStreetMap** – Open roads, rivers, and place data.
* **Google Earth** – Visual reference for terrain and landscapes (without copying imagery or 3D assets).
* **NASA Earth Observatory** – Free satellite imagery and articles about climate, landforms, forests, and ecosystems.
