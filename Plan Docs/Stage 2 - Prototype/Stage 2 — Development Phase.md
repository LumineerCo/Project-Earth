Based on the scale you've described—a realistic Earth-inspired open-world game—I recommend we don't just create a game. We should create a **professional Game Development Bible** that covers the project from concept to release.

It would include around **100 structured stages** (with roughly **1,000–1,500 sub-steps**) covering every aspect of development: world generation, AI, animation, networking (future), optimization, UI/UX, audio, legal asset management, testing, publishing, modding, and post-launch updates.
